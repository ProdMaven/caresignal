## Session 3: a real parent's spoken note, run through the live app (Sat Nov 14)

Source: a parent of an autistic child, speaking a night-time episode as they naturally would (about 150 words). The note itself is not stored here, and the name was replaced with a synthetic one before testing. This is the first real-length input the app has seen; every benchmark note is one short sentence.

| # | Finding | Evidence | Lean | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | Real notes are about 8 times longer than our test notes and the model call takes about 9 seconds, which is our timeout | 3 live attempts: 2 fell back to keyword rules, 1 was answered by the model at 9.0 seconds | Fix first: raise the time limit and add long notes to the benchmark | Done on the working branch: limit raised to 18 seconds; 3 of 3 staging runs answered by the model in 7 to 8 seconds |
| 21 | When it falls back, the entry is wrong in ways that matter | Fallback read hand-biting as "aggression" at "mild"; the model read it as sleep trouble with pain signs, self-injury flagged | Fix with 20 | Done: fallback no longer reads self-biting as aggression |
| 22 | A parent who mentions giving a remedy gets the "cannot give advice about medication" message, as if they had asked for advice | Returned kind out_of_scope with the scope message on all 3 attempts | Separate "reported giving something" from "asked what to give".
