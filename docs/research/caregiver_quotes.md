# Caregiver quotes

Real words from real caregivers, used with their agreement. Role only, no surnames. Do not add a quote here unless the person said it and agreed to share it.

## Quote source 1 (Nov 15, 2026)
- **Who:** a parent on our team, father of an autistic daughter.
- **Where the words come from:** a note he spoke into the app himself (voice to text) on Nov 15 about a hard moment the night before. These are his words as recorded, not an interview answer and not rewritten. He read the two quotes below and agreed to their use in the deck and in this repository.
- **What was happening:** the family was away from home for the night. His daughter became very upset and could not say what was wrong. He asked, she pointed, and he had to work it out from there. They went home.

## What this is and is not
- It is one parent, on our team, describing one night. It is not an interview study and must not be presented as one.
- It shows the core problem in his words: she could not say what was wrong, and he had to guess from a gesture.
- It does not, on its own, show the "each caregiver holds one piece" part of our story. That still needs interviews.
- Still to ask him, in his own words: "In that moment, what did you wish you knew?"
